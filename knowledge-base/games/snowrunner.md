## About the game

SnowRunner puts you in the driver’s seat of powerful vehicles as you conquer extreme open environments with the most advanced terrain simulation ever, by the makers of multi-million seller MudRunner.

Overcome mud, torrential waters, snow, and frozen lakes powered by Saber Interactive’s best-in-class terrain physics engine. Traverse wild environments inspired by North American and Eurasian wilderness that accurately react to your trucks’ weight, speed and momentum.

Drive 40 vehicles from brands such as Ford, Chevrolet, and Freightliner as you leave your mark on an untamed open world. Expand and customize your fleet with many upgrades and accessories including an exhaust snorkel for heavy waters or chain tires to battle the snow.
