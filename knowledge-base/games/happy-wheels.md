## About the game

Happy Wheels is a side-scrolling, physics-based, community driven sandbox game, now (finally) available, ad-free, on Steam. Select your inadequately prepared racer, and disregard severe consequences in your desperate pursuit of short-lived victory. Choose from more than 5 million user created levels or create and share your own.

**Features**

* Fully featured Level Editor -- build and share your own courses
* Play new, daily user submitted levels, sorted and ranked through the interactive Level Browser
* Multiple playable characters, each with unique vehicles and underwhelming abilities
* Life ending obstacles. Lack of adequate reward. Why even do this?
* ***NEW\****Level Browser Select Date feature, for deep dive into ancient levels of the forgotten past
* ***NEW\**** Unsavory Steam Achievements and Stats
* ***NEW\**** Link your old Happy Wheels account to your Steam ID, auto login on multiple devices

* ***NEW\**** Added controller support
* ***NEW\**** Download levels for offline play

* ***NEW\**** Complete lack of ads, I don't like ads
