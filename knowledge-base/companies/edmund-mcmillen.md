# Edmund McMillen

- **Wikipedia page:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edmund_McMillen
- **Description:** American video game designer and artist (born 1980)

## Overview

Edmund Charles John McMillen is an American video game designer and artist. He is known for his Adobe Flash games with unconventional visual styles. His works include the 2010 platformer Super Meat Boy, the 2011 roguelite game The Binding of Isaac, its 2014 remake The Binding of Isaac: Rebirth, and the 2026 turn-based roguelite game Mewgenics.
