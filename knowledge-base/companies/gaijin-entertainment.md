# Gaijin Entertainment

- **Wikipedia page:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gaijin_Entertainment
- **Description:** Hungarian video game developer

## Overview

Gaijin Entertainment is a Hungarian video game developer headquartered in Budapest. It was founded in Russia in 2002. The company is mostly known for War Thunder, Crossout, Star Conflict, CRSED: Cuisine Royale, Active Matter and Enlisted. Gaijin Entertainment is one of the founding members of the Cyprus game association.
