# NC (company)

- **Wikipedia page:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NC_(company)
- **Description:** South Korea-based video game developer and publisher

## Overview

NC Corporation, formerly known as NCSoft is a South Korean video game developer and publisher headquartered in Pangyo, Seongnam, South Korea, primarily known for the distribution of massively multiplayer online role-playing games such as Lineage and Guild Wars.
