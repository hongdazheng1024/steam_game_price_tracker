# Sonic Team

- **Wikipedia page:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sonic_Team
- **Description:** Japanese video game developer

## Overview

Sega CS R&D Dept. No. 2, doing business as Sonic Team, is a Japanese video game developer owned by Sega. Sonic Team is best known for its Sonic the Hedgehog series. Some Sonic Team games, such as the original Sonic games and Nights into Dreams (1996), are considered among the best ever made.
