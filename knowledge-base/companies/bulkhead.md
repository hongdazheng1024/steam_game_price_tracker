# Bulkhead (company)

- **Wikipedia page:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bulkhead_(company)
- **Description:** British video game developer

## Overview

Bulkhead is a British video game developer known for games like Wardogs and Battalion 1944. Bulkhead was founded in November 2015 in Derby, England, by University of Derby graduates Joe Brammer, David Jones, and Howard Philpott.
