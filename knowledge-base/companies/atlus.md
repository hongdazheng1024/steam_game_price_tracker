# Atlus

- **Wikipedia page:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atlus
- **Description:** Japanese video game company

## Overview

Atlus Co., Ltd.  is a Japanese video game developer, publisher, arcade manufacturer and distribution company based in Tokyo. A subsidiary of Sega, the company is known for the Megami Tensei, Persona, Etrian Odyssey, and Trauma Center series. Its corporate mascot is Jack Frost, a snowman-like character from its Megami Tensei series. The company is also known for its Print Club photo sticker arcade machines in East Asia.
