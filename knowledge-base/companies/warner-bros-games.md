# Warner Bros. Games

- **Wikipedia page:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warner_Bros._Games
- **Description:** American video game publisher

## Overview

Warner Bros. Games is an American video game publisher based in Burbank, California. The publisher was founded as a division of Warner Bros. on January 14, 2004, as well as the WB Games brand. Warner Bros. Games manages the wholly owned game development studios TT Games, Rocksteady Studios, NetherRealm Studios, WB Games Boston, Avalanche Software and WB Games Montréal among others.
