# Hello Games

- **Wikipedia page:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hello_Games
- **Description:** British video game developer

## Overview

Hello Games Ltd is a British video game company based in Guildford, Surrey. The company was founded by Sean Murray, Grant Duncan, Ryan Doyle and David Ream in February 2008 and has developed the Joe Danger series, No Man's Sky, The Last Campfire, and the upcoming Light No Fire.
